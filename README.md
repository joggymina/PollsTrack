# PollsTrack

**Polling Station Results Capture & Aggregation System for Kenya**

PollsTrack is an internal monitoring and transparency tool designed for party primaries and general elections in Kenya. It allows authorised agents at polling stations to submit results immediately after counting, while automatically aggregating them in real time from Polling Station → Ward → Constituency → County → National.

## Key Features

- **Station-level data capture** – Original results are stored independently and immutably
- **Real-time aggregation** – Automatic roll-up across all administrative levels
- **Offline capability** – Agents can record results even with poor or no internet
- **Secure submission** – Only authorised agents can submit for their assigned stations
- **Live dashboard** – Role-based views with progressive totals and drill-down
- **Photo evidence** – Option to attach a photo of the official results form
- **Full audit trail** – Who submitted what and when
- **Admin tools** – Manage stations, users, and monitor submission progress
